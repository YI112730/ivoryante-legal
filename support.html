Support

Ivory Ante is a landscape game. You field up to six decks. A deck is five cards bound to one god. Fights resolve both sides at once. The tower is 300 floors, in blocks of ten. Play does not need an account or a network.

Language and effects

On the title screen, choose English, 简体中文, Nederlands, Svenska, Norsk, or Dansk. The title stays “Ivory Ante”. Effects can be turned off on the same screen.

If a deck breaks

A Star Anchor can revive one deck once per block and restart that floor from the first fight. Anchors come from play. They are not a purchase.

Lost progress

The save stays on the device. Deleting the app deletes the save. There is no cloud save and no account to restore it.

Devices

iPhone and iPad, landscape only. iPad runs full screen.
