Privacy Policy

Effective 21 November 2026.

Ivory Ante does not collect data. The developer does not receive your gameplay, your device identifier, or a profile of you.

On the device

Progress, settings, and the chosen language stay in the app’s local save. That save is not uploaded. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

The game is a paid download. Apple handles the payment. The developer does not receive your payment details and does not sell anything inside the app. There are no ads.

Children

The game is not directed at children, and it does not ask for a name, an email, or an account.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text will be published on this page.

Contact

A contact email has not been published yet.
